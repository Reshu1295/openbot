### Added

- You can now talk with an agent by voice, as on the desktop. In an agent chat, tap the microphone in the message field. The agent listens until you stop speaking, sends what you said, and reads its answer aloud. Then it listens again. The round button mutes the microphone, the stop button interrupts the answer, and the close button ends the conversation.
- A new Voice page in Settings. Choose the voice that reads replies: Supertonic 3, the same voice as on the desktop, which you download once and which runs on your phone, or ElevenLabs with your own API key. You can choose a voice, listen to a sample, and remove the model or the key.

### Changed

- In an agent chat, the microphone now starts a voice conversation in place of dictation with review. When no voice is ready, it opens Settings > Voice. Channels and tasks still dictate a message that you can review before you send it.
