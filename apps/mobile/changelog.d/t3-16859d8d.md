### Added

- You can now talk with an agent by voice, as on the desktop. In an agent chat, tap the microphone in the message field. The agent listens until you stop speaking, sends what you said, and reads its answer aloud with the voice of your phone. Then it listens again. The round button mutes the microphone, the stop button interrupts the answer, and the close button ends the conversation. Speech is recognized and read on your phone.

### Changed

- In an agent chat, the microphone now starts a voice conversation in place of dictation with review. Channels and tasks still dictate a message that you can review before you send it.
